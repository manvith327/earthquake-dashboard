How EEW Works

Early warning systems detect fast-moving primary P-waves and provide a few precious seconds of warning before the slower, more destructive secondary S-waves (strong shaking) arrive. This allows autonomous systems to halt trains, shut down pipelines, and allows people to drop, cover, and hold on.

Safety Guide

🔥 During an Earthquake
  • Drop to your hands and knees.
  • Cover your head and neck under a sturdy desk.
  • Hold On to your shelter until shaking stops.
  • Stay away from glass and outside doors.
⚠️ After an Earthquake
  • Expect aftershocks. Drop, Cover, and Hold On.
  • Check for injuries and get first aid.
  • If inside a damaged building, evacuate quickly.
  • Use texts instead of calls to keep lines clear.
🛡️ Be Prepared (Before)
  • Secure heavy items to walls.
  • Create an emergency communications plan.
  • Prepare a 72-hour supply kit.
